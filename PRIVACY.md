# Privacy Policy — Google Photos Slideshow Styler

**Last updated: 2026-09-28**

Google Photos Slideshow Styler does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or other user information.

## What the extension does

The extension changes the visual presentation of the Google Photos slideshow: background, frame, mat, drop shadow, and an optional ambient light glow. It does not read, modify, upload, delete, or share your photos or any other content.

## Data storage

Your style settings and custom presets are saved locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

## Ambient light feature

When enabled, the extension's background service worker fetches a small copy of the currently displayed photo directly from Google's own image domains (`*.googleusercontent.com`, `*.usercontent.google.com`, `*.ggpht.com`) to sample its edge colors on-device. This is done only to render the glow effect locally; the image data is never sent anywhere else and is not retained.

## Third parties

The extension makes no network requests to any server other than Google's own photo-serving domains described above. No analytics, tracking, or advertising code is included.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
